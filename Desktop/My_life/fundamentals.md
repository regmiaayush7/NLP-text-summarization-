## 1. The nine grahas

Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu (north node), Ketu (south node). Uranus, Neptune and Pluto are not used.
Natural benefics: Jupiter, Venus, waxing Moon, unafflicted Mercury. Natural malefics: Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, waning Moon, Mercury joined with malefics.

Dignity order, strongest first: exalted > moolatrikona > own sign > great friend's > friend's > neutral > enemy's > great enemy's > debilitated. Debilitation is exactly 180° from exaltation.

Natural strength (naisargika): Saturn < Mars < Mercury < Jupiter < Venus < Moon < Sun.
Digbala (directional strength): Mercury, Jupiter in 1st; Sun, Mars in 10th; Saturn in 7th; Moon, Venus in 4th.
Moon, Mars, Saturn strong at night; Mercury always; others by day.

### Karakatvas (significations)
- Sun: soul, father, government, authority, ego, vitality, heart, bones, right eye.
- Moon: mind, mother, emotions, public, fluids, travel, sleep, left eye.
- Mars: younger siblings, courage, land/property, police/army, surgery, accidents, blood, energy.
- Mercury: intellect, speech, trade, writing, maths, maternal uncle, friends, skin, nerves.
- Jupiter: wisdom, teacher, children, a woman's husband, wealth, dharma, liver, fat.
- Venus: spouse (esp. a man's wife), love, marriage, vehicles, comfort, arts, beauty, kidneys, reproduction.
- Saturn: longevity, sorrow, servants/labour, delay, discipline, old age, chronic illness, legs.
- Rahu: foreigners, sudden events, obsession, poisons, technology, illusion, maternal grandparents.
- Ketu: spirituality, liberation, detachment, past karma, sudden loss, wounds, paternal grandparents.

## 2. Relationships and strength basics

### Natural friendship (BPHS)
| Graha | Friends | Neutral | Enemies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Moon, Mars, Jupiter | Mercury | Venus, Saturn |
| Moon | Sun, Mercury | Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn | – |
| Mars | Sun, Moon, Jupiter | Venus, Saturn | Mercury |
| Mercury | Sun, Venus | Mars, Jupiter, Saturn | Moon |
| Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mars | Saturn | Mercury, Venus |
| Venus | Mercury, Saturn | Mars, Jupiter | Sun, Moon |
| Saturn | Mercury, Venus | Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mars |
| Rahu, Ketu (common view) | Mercury, Venus, Saturn | Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mars |

Friendship is one-way (Moon likes Mercury; Mercury dislikes the Moon).
Temporary friends: planets in 2, 3, 4, 10, 11, 12 from a planet. Temporary enemies: 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.
Compound: friend+friend = great friend; neutral+friend = friend; enemy+friend or friend+enemy = neutral; neutral+enemy = enemy; enemy+enemy = great enemy.

### Combustion (asta)
Moon 12°; Mars 17°; Mercury 14° (12° retro); Jupiter 11°; Venus 10° (8° retro); Saturn 15° (some 16°). Nodes never combust. Mercury's combustion is usually treated lightly.

### Retrogression (vakri)
Sun and Moon never; nodes always. Retrograde = near Earth, bright, maximum Cheshta bala; acts with extra force, can give results like exaltation; retrograde in debilitation is often read as good, in exaltation as weaker; also gives results of the previous sign.

### Planetary war (graha yuddha)
Two of Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn within 1° of longitude. Winner: further north in latitude (Surya Siddhanta); else brighter, larger, faster. Venus usually wins. The loser's dasha gives weak results.

### Avasthas
- Baladi (odd signs): 0–6° Bala (~25%), 6–12° Kumara (~50%), 12–18° Yuva (full), 18–24° Vriddha (little), 24–30° Mrita (none). Reverse in even signs.
- Jagradadi: exalted/own = Jagrat (full); friend/neutral = Swapna (medium); enemy/debilitated = Sushupti (none).
- Deeptadi: Deepta (exalted), Swastha (own), Mudita (great friend), Shanta (friend), Deena (neutral), Dukhita (enemy), Vikala (with malefic), Khala (debilitated/great enemy), Kopa (combust).
- Lajjitadi: Lajjita, Garvita, Kshudita, Trushita, Mudita, Kshobhita.

### Lordship rules (Parashara)
1. Trikona lords always good.
2. Lords of 3, 6, 11 bad (increasing).
3. Kendradhipati dosha: natural benefic owning a kendra loses goodness (Jupiter, Venus most); natural malefic owning a kendra stops harming.
4. 8th lord bad unless also lagna lord; Sun and Moon exempt.
5. 2nd and 12th lords neutral; follow their other house and company.
6. Kendra + trikona lord = yogakaraka.
7. Rahu/Ketu give results of their sign lord and companions.
8. Marakas: lords of 2 and 7, planets in 2 and 7, planets with those lords; Saturn joined with a maraka becomes chief.

### Other house rules
- Bhavat bhavam: the house as far from a house as it is from lagna repeats its theme (9th from 9th = 5th).
- Derived houses: treat a relative's house as their lagna (4th from 4th = mother's home; 9th from 4th = maternal grandfather).
- Karaka bhava nashaya: karaka alone in its own house can spoil it (Jupiter in 5th, Venus in 7th, Moon in 4th); Saturn in 8th is the exception (long life).
- Benefics in kendras/trikonas protect; malefics in 3, 6, 11 help; any planet in 6, 8, 12 weakens unless it rules it.
- A house is strong when its lord is strong and well placed, benefics occupy or aspect it, and SAV ≥ 28.

## 5. Drishti (aspects)

| Graha | Full aspects (counted from itself, inclusive) |
|---|---|
| Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus | 7th |
| Mars | 4th, 7th, 8th |
| Jupiter | 5th, 7th, 9th |
| Saturn | 3rd, 7th, 10th |
| Rahu, Ketu | 5th, 7th, 9th in most modern practice (disputed) |

Partial (BPHS): ¼ on 3rd and 10th, ½ on 5th and 9th, ¾ on 4th and 8th (used in Drik bala).
Benefic aspect protects; malefic aspect damages; Jupiter's aspect is the strongest protection. Conjunction = same sign, stronger when closer. Sambandha (needed for most yogas): exchange, conjunction, mutual aspect, or one in the other's sign aspecting it.

Jaimini rashi drishti: movable signs aspect the fixed signs except the adjacent one; fixed aspect movable except adjacent; dual aspect the other duals.

Tajika (annual charts): degree-based; orbs Sun 15°, Moon 12°, Mars 8°, Mercury 7°, Jupiter 9°, Venus 7°, Saturn 9°. 3/11 and 5/9 friendly; 1/7 and 4/10 hostile.

## 6. Sidereal zodiac, ayanamsa, chart styles

Planets are measured against the fixed stars; the gap from Western (tropical) positions is the ayanamsa, growing ~50.3″/year (~1° per 72 years).

| Ayanamsa (2026.0) | Value | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Lahiri / Chitrapaksha (default, Govt of India 1955, Nepali panchangs) | 24.22° (24°13′ on 1 Jan 2026) | Spica at 180° |
| KP | 24.13° | Lahiri + Newcomb |
| Yukteshwar | 24.09° | Holy Science |
| Surya Siddhanta (revised) | 23.85° | Classical |
| Raman | 22.82° | B. V. Raman |
| Fagan-Bradley | 24.87° | Aldebaran 15° Taurus |

The ~2° spread can shift the lagna for about 1 chart in 10.

Houses: Parashari = whole sign; Bhava Chalit (Sripati) shifts planets near cusps; KP = Placidus.
Three reference lagnas: ascendant (body/life), Chandra lagna (mind; transits), Surya lagna (soul, status).

Chart styles: North Indian (diamond, houses fixed, top centre = house 1, sign numbers move; used in Nepal), South Indian (4×4 grid, signs fixed, Pisces top-left), East Indian (3×3, Aries top centre, anticlockwise).
