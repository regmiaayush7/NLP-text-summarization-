# The 27 nakshatras

Each nakshatra = 13°20′, four padas of 3°20′ (108 padas; each pada = one navamsa). Lords follow the dasha order Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, three times. The Moon's nakshatra (janma nakshatra) sets the Vimshottari dasha, the child's name syllable, and most of the marriage score.

## Everyday nakshatra rules
- Gana: Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa (nine each) – temperament; used in matching.
- Yoni sworn enemies: horse–buffalo, elephant–lion, sheep–monkey, serpent–mongoose, dog–deer, cat–rat, cow–tiger.
- Nadi: Adi (vata), Madhya (pitta), Antya (kapha). Same nadi in a couple = Nadi dosha.
- Muhurta classes: Fixed (Rohini, 3 Uttaras) – foundations, coronations. Movable (Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana, Dhanishtha, Shatabhisha) – travel, vehicles. Fierce (Bharani, Magha, 3 Purvas) – aggressive acts. Mixed (Krittika, Vishakha). Swift (Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta, Abhijit) – trade, medicine, travel. Soft (Mrigashira, Chitra, Anuradha, Revati) – marriage, arts, friendship. Sharp (Ardra, Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Mula) – surgery, exorcism, separation.
- Gandamula: Moon in Ashwini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Revati – Mula shanti on the 27th day.
- Panchaka: Moon from second half of Dhanishtha through Revati (Aquarius–Pisces) – avoid starting a roof, southward travel, collecting firewood, cremation without special rites.
- Tara bala: count from birth nakshatra to the day's nakshatra, remainder after dividing by 9: 1 Janma, 2 Sampat (wealth), 3 Vipat (danger), 4 Kshema (well-being), 5 Pratyak (obstacle), 6 Sadhana (achievement), 7 Naidhana (death), 8 Mitra, 9 Parama Mitra. Avoid 3, 5, 7 for important work.
- Name from nakshatra: the nwaran name begins with the pada syllable above.
