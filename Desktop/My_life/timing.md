# Timing: dashas and gochara (transits)

The birth chart shows what is promised; the dasha shows when; the transit delivers it. Predict an event only when all three agree.

Balance at birth = lord's years × (unfinished part of the Moon's nakshatra ÷ 13°20′).
Antardasha years = MD years × AD lord's years ÷ 120 (starts with the MD lord itself). Pratyantardasha = AD length × PD lord's years ÷ 120.
Software differs by a few days depending on year length (365.25 vs 365.2425 days) and ayanamsa.

### Rules for judging a dasha
1. The MD lord gives the results of the houses it owns, the house it occupies, its karakatvas, and planets joining/aspecting it.
2. Dignity and strength set quality: exalted, own-sign, yogakaraka lords → good periods; debilitated, combust, dusthana lords → trouble unless cancelled.
3. Read the AD lord from the MD lord: 6/8 or 2/12 apart = friction; kendras/trikonas from each other = cooperation.
4. Rahu and Ketu give results of their sign lord, companions and house.
5. A planet gives the results of the nakshatra lord it sits in (basis of KP).
6. Yogas fructify in the dashas of their planets.
7. Maraka (2nd/7th lord) dashas are checked for health crises (never announce death timing).
8. Dasha sandhi (last months of one MD, first of the next) is unsettled.
9. Confirm with transits: Jupiter and Saturn both touching the relevant house or lord (double transit).

| Mercury | ~1 month | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11 | 5, 3, 9, 1, 8, 12 |

| Rahu, Ketu | ~18 months | 3, 6, 11 | 12, 9, 5 |

No vedha between Sun and Saturn, or Moon and Mercury.

Transit rules
- Double transit (K. N. Rao): major events when Jupiter and Saturn both transit/aspect the house or its lord, inside a supporting dasha.
- Ashtakavarga filter: 4+ own BAV bindus in the transit sign = good even in a "bad" house; few bindus spoil a "good" one.
- Murti nirnaya: when Jupiter, Saturn or Rahu changes sign, note the transiting Moon from natal Moon: 1, 6, 11 gold (best); 2, 5, 9 silver; 3, 7, 10 copper; 4, 8, 12 iron (hard).
- Transits over natal planets and the lagna trigger them; transits of the running dasha lord matter most.
- Saturn/Jupiter in 8th or 12th from the Moon and Sade Sati are classic hard periods.
- Eclipses on the birth nakshatra, natal luminaries or lagna are unfavourable for months.
- Retrograde transits repeat their effect over the same degrees.
